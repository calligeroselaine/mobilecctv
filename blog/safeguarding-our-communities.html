Safeguarding Our Communities Whilst Protecting Our Youth

As a community, we all share the responsibility of ensuring the safety and well-being of our youth. However, recent incidents of antisocial behaviour in youth skate parks and other gathering areas have raised concerns about the safety of our community.

At Mobile CCTV Solutions, we understand the importance of proactive measures in preventing and addressing antisocial behaviour. That’s why councils all around Australia are turning to our cutting-edge pole camera technology and security trailers to protect neighbourhoods and deter criminal activity.

Our pole camera technology is a powerful tool in the fight against antisocial behaviour. By strategically placing these cameras in key locations, councils can monitor activity in real-time, gather evidence for prosecution, and create a safer environment for young people to enjoy activities such as skating without fear, intimidation or assault.

What sets our security trailers apart is their unparalleled mobility and ease of use. They can be easily towed by any vehicle with a tow ball and set up in minutes by a single operator, meaning they can be deployed quickly to areas experiencing heightened levels of antisocial behaviour.

We’re committed to ensuring councils have access to the resources they need to make informed decisions about their security measures. Call us on 1300 99 69 10 to discover how Mobile CCTV Solutions can tailor our offerings to your council’s specific needs, or visit us at our offices in Sydney’s Northern Beaches for a personalised demonstration.

Already partnering with regional and metro councils and businesses across Australia, Mobile CCTV Solutions plays a vital role in combating this behaviour and providing a safer environment for community members.
