Protecting Utility Sites from Copper Theft

⚠ For client review: This post had no title on the original site (it displayed blank in the blog title/browser tab). We’ve given it a title based on its content — please confirm this is the intended headline.

Mobile CCTV Solutions is working with a utility supplier that has reported break-ins occurring at their sites, along with copper being stolen.

As well as the huge cost to the company associated with replacing the copper, the live plants are extremely dangerous and pose a risk to intruders.

To overcome this ongoing issue of break-ins and theft, we are building two new CCTV trailers that will easily integrate with our client’s existing security system, allowing the trailers to be quickly dispatched and respond to hot spots. Not only do the trailers act as a physical deterrent, they assist with identifying offenders and ceasing the theft.

Mobile CCTV trailers can be easily moved with any towable vehicle and are set up in minutes. Our trailers have easily operated cameras for you to view and drive from your phone or office.

Contact us for a quick online remote demonstration so we can show you the trailers live in action and how they can help your site.

This month we have trailers available for immediate deployment for either short or long-term hire, starting at 1 day. Contact us today for same-week delivery.
