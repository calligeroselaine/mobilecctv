Mobile CCTV Solutions was proud to support the recent Granny Smith Festival in Ryde, providing high-tech, flexible security to keep the event safe and enjoyable for all. With hundreds of stalls, performances, and thousands of attendees, strong security was essential. Our mobile CCTV units were on the ground, adapting to the unique needs of this busy, vibrant festival.

Our mobile CCTV units were strategically placed across festival zones like the food stalls, youth area, kids' activities, and main stages. With high-definition cameras and built-in lighting, they provided clear footage day and night, helping organisers manage crowd flow and quickly address any issues.

The ease and flexibility of our units meant we could provide comprehensive coverage without interfering with the event experience. Set up where needed, they provided organisers with immediate access to live footage, enhancing response times and supporting a smooth, safe event.

Mobile CCTV Solutions is committed to backing community events with reliable, mobile security that fits any environment, from local festivals to large gatherings. With high-quality video, remote monitoring, and adaptability, our units offer peace of mind, allowing organisers and attendees to focus on what matters — celebrating community and tradition.
