As a community, we all share the responsibility of ensuring the safety and well-being of our youth. However, recent incidents of antisocial behaviour in youth skate parks and other gathering areas have raised concerns about the safety of our community and the integrity of some residents.

At the weekend, a 10 year old was allegedly snatched at a skatepark in Woodridge in Queensland. The skate park in Miranda NSW also allegedly saw an assault at the weekend, and while skateparks are there for kids to let off some steam, they can be a place for antisocial and often dangerous behaviour.

At Mobile CCTV Solutions, we understand the importance of proactive measures in preventing and addressing antisocial behaviour. That's why councils all around Australia are turning to our cutting-edge pole camera technology and security trailers to protect our neighbourhoods and deter criminal activity.

Our pole camera technology is a powerful tool in the fight against antisocial behaviour. By strategically placing these cameras in key locations, councils can monitor activity in real-time, gather evidence for prosecution, and ultimately create a safer environment for our youth to enjoy activities such as skating on ramps without fear, intimidation or assault. Not only do youth feel safer, but loiterers are also deterred from engaging in antisocial behaviour as they know they are being monitored and can easily be prosecuted.

What sets our security trailers apart is their unparalleled mobility and ease of use. These trailers can be easily towed by any vehicle with a tow ball and set up in minutes by a single operator. This means that they can be deployed quickly to areas experiencing heightened levels of antisocial behaviour, providing an immediate deterrent to potential troublemakers.
