Immediate Security and Live Monitoring for Flood-Affected Areas

With rising flood waters and copious amounts of flood damage, Local Councils are using our Mobile CCTV trailers to aid their disaster recovery and to prevent looting of vulnerable properties and businesses.

Mobile CCTV trailers are providing immediate security and live monitoring of flood-affected areas.

Our mobile CCTV units are easily deployed by one person and robust enough to travel along flood-affected roads. Our trailers can be operational within just minutes.

The CCTV cameras can be controlled and live-viewed remotely via any internet connection.
